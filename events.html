Events

Tierralta, Córdoba, is a vibrant and celebratory municipality where cultural events, festivals, and community activities reflect the joy, identity, and traditions of its people.
Throughout the year, locals and visitors can enjoy a diverse program that includes music, dance, gastronomy, sports, and opportunities for social interaction. These events not only strengthen the culture of the Alto Sinú region but also promote tourism and community development.
