Traditions

The traditions of Tierralta are the result of its indigenous heritage and Caribbean cultural influence. On the one hand, the ancestral customs of the Embera Katio people remain alive, preserving their rituals, dances, and traditional way of life.
On the other hand, music and dances such as the fandango, cumbia, and vallenato are part of the community’s daily life, especially during celebrations and festivities. Religious events like Holy Week also reflect the faith and sense of unity of its inhabitants.

Fandango

The fandango is one of the region's most representative cultural expressions, performed by folk bands and featured prominently in parades, dance troupes, and popular celebrations.

Cumbia

Cumbia is a dance that combines rhythm, elegance, and symbolism. It is danced in pairs with a courtship style, where the woman, wearing a wide skirt, makes circular movements while holding lit candles.

Vallenato

Vallenato is an emblematic musical genre of the Caribbean region that also has a strong presence in Tierralta, as it is performed with traditional instruments such as the accordion, the caja, and the guacharaca.
