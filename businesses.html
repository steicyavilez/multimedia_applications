Businesses

Tierralta boasts a wide variety of culinary businesses that reflect the creativity and hard work of its people. You’ll find creativity and hard work in its people. You’ll find everything from traditional restaurants to ice cream shops, bakeries, fried food stands, and fast-food outlets-there are options for all tastes and budgets.
These establishments offer more than just food; they provide experiences to share with family and friends while enjoying the local atmosphere.

Rico Pan Bakery

This well-known bakery is famous for its variety of traditional fried foods such as cheese sticks, empanadas, fritters, panzerotti, and potatoes. They also sell different types of bread and pastries.
In addition, it functions as a shop, offering drinks, arepas, and snacks, making it an ideal place to enjoy a typical breakfast or a quick snack.

La Perla Restaurant

This restobar offers a combination of dishes such as sushi, hamburgers, seafood, and other options, accompanied by alcoholic beverages or games, in a pleasant, family-friendly, and romantic atmosphere.
It features an outdoor space decorated with trees, lighting, and a nautical-themed mural, making it a perfect spot to share a meal or enjoy good food.

Ice Cream Shop, The Ice Cream Corner

This place is ideal for those looking for something sweet and refreshing, as it offers a wide variety of ice creams with combinations of fruits, cereals, cookies, and sauces.
It also has milkshakes and, in the evenings, fast food options, catering to different tastes and budgets.

Chicken Restaurant, Riki Chicken

Specializing in roasted chicken, this restaurant is known for its juicy and crispy flavor.
It also offers portions ranging from quarters to whole chickens, accompanied by potatoes, arepas, or bollos, along with a variety of sauces. Delivery service is also available, making it easy to access their products.

Restaurant, El Sazón Criollo

This restaurant specializes in traditional regional cuisine, offering dishes such as fish sancocho, bocachico prepared in various ways, meat, beans, and chicken stewed in coconut.
It’s an excellent option for those who want to experience the authentic flavors of Tierralta in a comfortable and welcoming atmosphere.
