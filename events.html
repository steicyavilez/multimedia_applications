Events in Tierralta

Festivals

The festivals of Tierralta are vibrant, characterized by folklore, fandango, comparsas (street dance groups), colorful parades, and livestock traditions. They highlight the culture of the Alto Sinú region, and thousands of people gather during these festivities, eager to enjoy and participate in these events.

1. Fandango de Reyes (Three Kings’ Day Festival)

This Fandango de Reyes festival, held in Tierralta in January, is a massive cultural celebration known for its comparsa parades, the pollera dance, band performances, vueltiao hats, candlelit dances, and children’s fandango.

2. Corralejas Festival

This festival, held in March in honor of Saint Joseph, is renowned for its high-quality bullfighting events, artistic performances such as fandango, concerts, artistic demonstrations, and large audiences.

3. Tierralta Anniversary

This festival is celebrated in November and commemorates the anniversary of Tierralta with large, free concerts and artistic, cultural, and recreational activities for the whole family. During this festival, you’ll hear vallenato, tropical, and folk music.

4. Tourism and Culture Festival

During this festival, there are discussions about community-based tourism, artistic displays, food fairs featuring local flavors, dance performances, craft exhibitions, and parades. All of this is aimed at promoting sustainable tourism, cultural heritage, and the biodiversity of the municipality of Tierralta.

Community Events

Tierralta hosts a variety of community events, which aim to foster community spirit and active participation. These events are typically free or low-cost and open to the public. They often include concerts, festivals, workshops, sports, and recreational activities, among other things.

1. Golden Half Marathon

This Golden Half Marathon was a community event held to support Macondo and its dream of creating a place where illness doesn’t exist. Thanks to the support of everyone who joined the cause, funds were raised for the construction of the Macondo Children’s Home, a refuge for children with cancer and their caregivers.

2. Gastronomic Festival: Knowledge and Stives

This gastronomic festival, “Knowledge and Stoves,” features restaurant competitions and heritage cuisine contests. There is also music, a buffet of Sinú cuisine, and a small farmer’s market.

3. Workshops at the Municipal Library

These workshops were held for children between 8 and 16 years old and included dance, reading, theater, painting, and music workshops for this age group.

4. Comfacor Activities

The Córdoba Family Compensation Fund-Comfacor-also offers various activities for children and young people, including painting workshops, cultural and recreational festivals, sports activities, and crafts.
