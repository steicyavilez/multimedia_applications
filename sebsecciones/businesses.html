Gastronomy of Tierralta

Typical Dishes

Typical Colombia dishes are homemade, hearty, and delicious. They are characterized by the inclusion of rice, plantains, yucca, potatoes, beans, or corn, and are accompanied by pork, chicken, or beef. Fish or eggs may also be included, and salad is a common accompaniment.

1. Mote de Queso (Cheese Soup)

This typical dish from Tierralta is a soup made with diced yam and cheese, seasoned with red onion, garlic, and lemon. White onion, scallions, cilantro, tomato, or whey can also be added. It can be served with rice, chicharrón (fried pork rinds), avocado, fried plantain slices, patacón (fried green plantain), and aguapanela (a drink made with unrefined cane sugar).

2. Bocachico (Fish)

This traditional dish from Tierralta is very popular, as it is a local fish known for its delicious flavor. It can be eaten fried or in soup and is served with rice, plantains, yucca, yam, salad, fried plantain slices, avocado, and other accompaniments.

3. Machucado

Machucado, also known as cabeza de gato (cat’s head), is another traditional dish from Tierralta. It’s primarily eaten for breakfast and consists of cooked plantains, which are then mashed and served either as is or in the form of small balls. Ingredients such as oil, onion, and garlic are added. This delicious dish can be accompanied by a wide variety of toppings, including eggs, cheese, butter, pork rinds, ribs, sausage, chicken, stew, and chorizo.

Businesses

The municipality of Tierralta boasts a wide variety of restaurants, as well as ice cream shops, a rotisserie chicken restaurant, a bakery, a confectionery, fruit stands, fast food vendors, fried food stands, stationery stores, workshops, laundromats, and more.

1. Rico Pan Bakery

This bakery and delicatessen are well-known for their fried foods, including cheese sticks, potatoes with meat, meat empanadas, jaguayana empanadas, fritters, and chicken pie. It also functions as a convenience store, selling sodas, arepas, snacks, and other items.

2. La Perla Restaurant

In addition to fast food, this restaurant also offers grilled meats. It features excellent service and delicious food, perfect for enjoying with family or friends. This beautiful establishment has an outdoor area with trees, lights, and a mural with a maritime theme.

3. Ice Cream Shop, The Ice Cream Corner

This ice cream shop offers a variety of ice cream flavors, shapes, and combinations, such as ice cream with fruit, cereal, cookies, candy, and sauces. They also sell milkshakes in different flavors and fast food in the evenings. Because of this, they have a range of prices to suit different budgets.

4. Chicken Restaurant, Riki Chicken

This rotisserie chicken restaurant sells juicy, crispy, and delicious rotisserie chickens. You can buy the chicken whole, half, or quarter, depending on how many people are eating. It comes with potatoes, bread rolls or arepas, and various sauces. They also offer delivery if you can’t go in.

5. Olafo Store

This store and discount shop sells a wide variety of items, including stationery, school supplies, furniture, household goods, women’s, men’s, and children’s clothing, toiletries and makeup, toys, and much more.

6. Restaurant, El Sazón Criollo

This restaurant serves traditional food from Tierralta, including fish sancocho, bocachico sinuano stewed in coconut milk, fried bocachico, chicharron, pork with onions, grilled chicken breast, fried beef, beans with beef shank, ground beef, chicken stewed in coconut milk, and other delicious lunches.
