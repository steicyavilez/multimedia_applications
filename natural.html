Natural Tourist Route

This route is designed for those who wish to disconnect from the fast pace of daily life and reconnect with nature. It offers spaces surrounded by lush vegetation, fresh air, and natural sounds that provide a sense of peace and well-being.
Tierralta boasts diverse natural settings ideal for rest, contemplation, and responsible ecotourism, where you can enjoy unique landscapes and experience a revitalizing adventure.

Saltillo Tuis Tuis

This natural treasure of Tierralta is perfect for those seeking tranquility and direct contact with water and nature. Its crystal-clear waters, accompanied by a beautiful waterfall, create a cool and relaxing atmosphere ideal for unwinding.
The sound of the cascading water, the surrounding vegetation, and the purity of the environment make this a perfect place to disconnect, take photographs, and enjoy a moment of calm.

Saltillo La Oscurana

Reaching this destination is part of the experience, as the journey includes a road surrounded by natural landscapes, where you’ll cross bridges and small rivers teeming with biodiversity.
Upon arriving at a farm, you’ll need to leave your vehicle and continue the journey on foot, transforming the trip into an ecological adventure. Finally, you’ll find natural pools that reward your effort with a cool, tranquil, and visually stunning environment.

Green Energy Route

This route combines tourism and learning, allowing you to discover sustainable projects and the responsible use of natural resources in the region. It also includes visits to the URRA reservoir, where you can learn about hydroelectric power generation and its environmental impact. You’ll also explore the Alto Sinú Tourist Park, the Las Guartinajas Ecological Station, and the Ecolagos tourist complex. All of these places are ideal for those who want to understand the relationship between development, the environment, and sustainability.
