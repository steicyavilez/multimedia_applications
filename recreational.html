Recreational Tourist Route

This route is designed for those seeking fun, physical activity, and spaces for social interaction. Here, you can enjoy a variety of outdoor activities such as sports, games, and water experiences.
The included destinations offer areas for playing soccer and volleyball; activities like kayaking, boat rides, and paddle boats; as well as places for camping, hosting events, and participating in educational workshops about local flora and fauna.

Saltillo el Loro (The Parrot Waterfall)

This recreational site combines nature and entertainment in one place. It features a river ideal for swimming and spending time with family and friends, as well as shallow areas for added safety.
It offers hammocks, rest areas, and a playground. You can also enjoy typical meals and snacks, making it a perfect destination for spending a full day.

Ecolagos

Ecolagos is one of Tierralta’s most complete tourist destinations, boasting a large lake where you can enjoy activities such as canoeing, boating, kayaking, and paddle boating. It also offers a lookout point, relaxation areas with hammocks, picnic areas, sports courts, and recreational areas. Additionally, it features cabins, camping areas, a restaurant, beverage service, and spaces for events such as weddings, quinceañeras, and camps, making it an ideal place for both relaxation and fun.

Guartinajas

This ecological space is ideal for nature lovers and those interested in environmental learning. It offers guided tours where you can learn about the local flora and fauna in depth. Educational workshops are also held to help you understand the importance of ecosystem conservation. It’s a perfect destination for those who want an enriching and educational experience in direct contact with the natural environment.
