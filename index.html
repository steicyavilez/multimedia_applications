Exploring Tierralta

On this web application, you’ll find tourist routes, information on culture, gastronomy, and events in the municipality of Tierralta, Córdoba. All of this is designed to help you learn more about this beautiful municipality, its flora and fauna, typical dishes, festivals, community events, and much more, sparking your curiosity and adventurous spirit.
