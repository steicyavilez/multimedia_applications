Tourist Routes of Tierralta

Recreational Tourist Route

A recreational tourist route allows you to enjoy nature and have fun at the same time. These areas include playgrounds for soccer and volleyball, as well as kayaking, boating, paddleboats, camping, and event spaces. Workshops are also offered to learn more about the flora and fauna of Tierralta.

1. Saltillo el Loro (The Parrot Waterfall)

This recreational waterfall not only features a river for swimming, relaxing, and having fun with family and friends, but also hammocks, shallow areas of the river, a lookout point, and a playground. You can also enjoy a delicious lunch and snacks at this location.

2. Ecolagos

In the tourist area, you’ll find a large lake where you can swim, canoe, boat, paddleboat, and kayak. It also features a lookout point, hammocks, a spacious picnic area, a soccer field, and a volleyball court. There’s also a large patio for other recreational activities. Additionally, there are cabins, campsites, a restaurant, snacks, drinks, bike paths, and you can rent space for weddings, quinceañeras, camps, challenges, and other events.

3. Guartinajas

This ecological site boasts a large natural area where, in addition to tours and explanations about the local flora and fauna, workshops are offered. These workshops will help you learn more about the different species found there. This makes it a perfect spot for nature lovers who want to learn more about the natural world.
